Geographic & Operational Superiority

The Akwa Ibom Strategic Maritime Hub

Positioned at the epicenter of the Gulf of Guinea and Niger Delta coastal corridor, Akwa Ibom State represents Nigeria’s most promising frontier for blue-economy industrialization. PRIME-E leverages this natural maritime gateway to deliver unprecedented turnaround speed, deep-water logistics access, and regional supply chain security.

01

Deep Natural Draft

Extensive 129km Atlantic coastline with deep-draft natural channels accommodating modern commercial vessels, tugs, and trawler fleets without excessive tidal delays.

02

Gulf of Guinea Gateway

Direct maritime line-of-sight to offshore energy fields, international bunkering routes, and regional transshipment zones across Central and West Africa.

03

Local Job Creation

Empowering host communities in Ibeno, Oron, Eastern Obolo, and Ikot Abasi through specialized maritime apprenticeships, safety drills, and artisanal fishery off-take.

04

Port & Jetty Infrastructure

Strategic connectivity to the Ibom Deep Seaport industrial zone, riverine jetties, and multi-modal road transit lines linking the hinterland.
